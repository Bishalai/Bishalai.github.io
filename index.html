01 About

Engineering first, measured always.

I'm a machine learning engineer based in Kathmandu, Nepal. My work sits at the intersection of generative AI and applied computer vision — parameter-efficient fine-tuning of large language and diffusion models, and end-to-end pipelines that cover dataset curation, training, evaluation, and inference.

Recent projects include conditioning 3× digital-elevation-model super-resolution on Sentinel-2 imagery across more than 40 sites in High Mountain Asia, and a real-time player-tracking system that turns match footage into tactical insight. I care about systems that are reproducible, benchmarked, and genuinely useful — not demos that only work once.

I hold a Bachelor of Computer Engineering from Pulchowk Campus (IOE, Tribhuvan University) and am currently reading for a Master of Data Science at Tribhuvan University. Outside of research I work as an assistant tour leader and trekking guide, play guitar live, and write about the models and systems I build.

03 Selected work

Things I've built

Select a project to read the details.

  • Reduced trainable parameters by over 90% via LoRA while retaining full task performance on fine-tuned LLMs and Stable Diffusion models.
  • Delivered end-to-end pipelines — dataset curation, training, evaluation, and ComfyUI inference — with systematic rank/alpha sweeps to optimise image fidelity vs. compute cost.

90%+fewer trainable parameters

  • 3× digital elevation model super-resolution: upsampling a 30 m NASADEM to 10 m, conditioned on the co-registered Sentinel-2 RGB image.
  • Benchmarked over 40 named sites across High Mountain Asia to test how well the model generalises across radically different terrain.

30 m → 10 m3× upsampling · 40+ sites

  • Achieved 93% accuracy in automated match-statistics generation by building a real-time player-and-ball tracking pipeline on live footage.
  • Generated positional time-series visualisations adopted for tactical post-match analysis.

93%tracking accuracy
